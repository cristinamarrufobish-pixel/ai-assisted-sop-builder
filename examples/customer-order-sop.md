# Example: AI-Assisted Customer Order SOP

## Project goal

This example demonstrates how unstructured operational notes can be transformed into a clearer Standard Operating Procedure with AI assistance and human validation.

The scenario is fictional and intentionally generic. It contains no real customer data, company procedures, account information, or proprietary systems.

## Input: unstructured notes

```text
Customer says order has not arrived.
Check order status.
Confirm address.
Check tracking.
If carrier says delivered but customer did not receive it, document what happened.
Ask customer to confirm safe locations.
If still missing, escalate based on company policy.
Update customer before closing the case.
```

## AI task

The AI is asked to:

- Preserve the meaning of the original notes.
- Organize them into a logical sequence.
- Use clear language suitable for a new team member.
- Add headings and a validation checkpoint.
- Avoid inventing company policies, timelines, refund rules, or escalation criteria that were not supplied.

## Draft output

### Process: Investigating a Missing Customer Order

**Purpose**

Provide a consistent approach for investigating a customer's report that an expected order has not arrived.

**Procedure**

1. Open the order record and review the current status and expected delivery information.
2. Confirm the delivery address with the customer.
3. Review the carrier tracking information and most recent scan.
4. If tracking indicates the package was delivered, ask the customer to check common delivery locations and whether another person at the address may have received it.
5. Document the tracking status and information provided by the customer.
6. If the order remains missing, follow the organization's approved escalation process.
7. Give the customer a clear update about the status and next step before ending the interaction.

**Validation checkpoint**

Before escalating or completing the case, verify that the delivery address, tracking information, customer response, and actions taken have been documented.

## Human review

Before considering the SOP complete, I would check:

1. **Fidelity:** Every required action from the source notes is represented.
2. **No invented policy:** The draft does not create refund amounts, deadlines, replacement rules, or escalation criteria.
3. **Clarity:** A new employee could understand the sequence.
4. **Documentation:** The process includes a clear recordkeeping checkpoint.
5. **Privacy:** No customer or company-specific information appears in the example.

## What AI improved

AI made the notes easier to scan, grouped related actions into a logical order, and converted shorthand into consistent instructions.

## What still requires a person

A human process owner must verify the operational accuracy, determine the actual company escalation policy, confirm compliance requirements, and approve the SOP before it is used in a real workflow.

## Key lesson

The value of AI in this workflow is not that it knows the company's process. The value is that it can accelerate organization and drafting while a knowledgeable person remains responsible for accuracy, context, privacy, and the final decision.
